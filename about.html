01. The Beginning

HOW A REFURBISHED VAN BECAME A MOVEMENT

In 2021, fed up with frozen commercial burgers and bland fast-food chains, Chef Marcus Vance bought a retired 1998 step van, ripped out the interior, and welded in a custom high-output cast iron flat top.

On opening night in Koramangala, we sold out all 120 smash burgers in 42 minutes flat. The queue stretched around two blocks, and STACKLY was born. Today, our fleet rolls across four major cities, but our core conviction remains untouched: craft street food with zero compromise.

03. Culinary Ethos

FRESHNESS. CRUST. CONSISTENCY.

A smash burger isn't just about pressing meat onto iron. It's about thermodynamics. We grind whole cuts in-house daily with an exact 80/20 fat ratio, smashing the beef within seconds onto searing 450°F cast iron plates.

This creates an intensely savory caramelized crust (the Maillard reaction) while keeping the interior succulent and beefy. Every sauce is balanced to cut through richness with tangy acidity, smoked spices, and umami depth.

06. Engineering & Ops

INSIDE THE MOBILE POWERHOUSE

Each STACKLY truck is a custom-fabricated culinary vessel featuring dual commercial exhaust canopies, stainless food-grade surfaces, commercial deep fryers, and onboard refrigerated staging.

  • Zero Generator Noise: Powered by hybrid silent lithium battery packs.
  • Rapid Output: Capable of pumping out 180 hot burgers per hour during peak rushes.
  • Hospital-grade Sanitation: Daily ozone disinfection and thermal water sanitizing.
